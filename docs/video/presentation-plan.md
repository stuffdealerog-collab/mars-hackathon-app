# Mercat video presentation: founder and teammates on camera (after the ad)

**Format.** The 41 s ad plays first (`docs/video/ad/storyboard.md`). Then 2–3 minutes of the team speaking on camera. These are **real people** (AI presenters were ruled out), cut with the product's screen recordings and shots from the ad.
**Language.** Speakers talk in the language they're most natural in (RU or UZ), with burned-in subtitles in the other.

## Filming it well with phones (≈30 min for the whole team)
- **Camera:** the best phone available, rear camera, 4K at 25 fps, locked exposure and focus (long-press in the camera app). Use a tripod or a stack of books, with the lens at eye level.
- **Light:** face a window at about 45°, with no window behind the speaker. Turn off overhead lights so there's one clean source.
- **Sound:** this matters more than the picture. Use a lav mic into a second phone in the speaker's pocket, or the phone 50 cm from the mouth in a quiet room (soft furniture, no echo). Clap once at the start of every take so audio can be synced.
- **Framing:** medium close-up (chest up), eyes on the upper third, a little air above the head. Background: a plain wall, or the venue softly out of focus.
- **Delivery:** use a teleprompter app on the phone with short sentences. Do two takes per part and keep the more natural one. Talk to one person, not to "the audience".
- **B-roll (5 min):** hands on the laptop running the real product, the team at the table, the venue (Humo Arena). This covers jump cuts.

## Edit
- Timeline 1920×1080, 25 fps. Use one grade for everything and the same grain as the ad.
- Lower thirds in the Mercat style: white sans on an ink pill with a lime dot. Name and role, on screen for 3 s.
- Music under the talking at −24 to −28 LUFS. Voices at about −14 LUFS integrated.
- Subtitles: SRT from the speakers' scripts, burned in, lower third, 2 lines maximum.
